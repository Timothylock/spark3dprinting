Est. 2020

Made by people who can't stop printing.

Spark3DPrinting started in 2020 with a single printer and a long list of things we wanted to exist. Then came a second printer, a shelf of filament, and a lot of late nights watching first layers go down.

Somewhere along the way the hobby turned into a small shop. We've since designed, printed, and shipped thousands of orders - every one of them made by people who genuinely love this stuff.

The files are free if you want to print them yourself. If you'd rather skip the printing, finished pieces are in the Etsy shop.
